How it works

Booking a chair should take less time than finding parking

No calling around, no DMing a salon and waiting for a reply. Here's exactly what happens from open to app to appointment.

01 · Discover

Browse verified stylists

Search by service, style, price range or distance. Every listing has real photos of past work and honest reviews from other clients.

02 · Compare

See prices upfront

No hidden fees, no "depends on your hair" surprises at the till. Every price shown is what you pay, before you book.

03 · Book

Pick a slot that's actually open

Availability updates live from the stylist's own calendar. What you see is what you get — no double-bookings.

04 · Confirm

Instant confirmation

Your stylist is notified immediately. You get a confirmation with the address, timing and a reminder closer to the day.

05 · Prepare

Reminders that actually help

A nudge the day before, and one an hour out — so you're never the reason a chair sits empty.

06 · Sit down

Walk in, no waiting

Your chair is held and your stylist is ready. Skip the walk-in line entirely, and rebook before you even leave.
