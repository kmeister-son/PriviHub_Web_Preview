Vendor Terms

The agreement between PriviHub and the stylists, barbers, salons and spas who list on it.

1. Parties and acceptance

1.1This agreement is between PriviHub (Pty) Ltd, registration number [CIPC number], [address] ("PriviHub") and the person or business that applies to list services on PriviHub ("you" or the "vendor").

1.2You accept this agreement by ticking the acceptance box in the vendor application. It takes effect when PriviHub approves your application. The Privacy Policy and the Booking, Cancellation, Refund and Safety Policy (the "Booking Policy") are part of it.

1.3You must be 18 or older, entitled to work in South Africa, and able to provide the services you list.

2. Our role

2.1PriviHub provides a marketplace that connects you with customers and handles bookings and payments. PriviHub does not provide beauty, hair or spa services and does not supervise or direct how you perform them.

2.2You are an independent business. Nothing in this agreement makes you an employee, partner or agent of PriviHub. You decide your own prices, hours, methods and equipment, and may work on other platforms, subject to clause 9.

2.3When a customer books you, the contract for that service is between you and the customer. You are the supplier for purposes of the Consumer Protection Act and responsible for complying with it.

3. Applying and being verified

3.1To apply you must give truthful, current information and the documents we request. For individual vendors this is your name, mobile number, email, South African ID number, a photo of your ID document and a selfie. For registered businesses it also includes your company registration number, registration documents and, if registered, your VAT number.

3.2Biometric consent. The selfie is compared with your ID photo to confirm that you are the person on the ID. This is special personal information under POPIA. By giving the selfie you consent to us processing it only for this purpose, and to keeping it for the period in the Privacy Policy. You may withdraw consent, but we then cannot approve or keep your listing.

3.3We review each application and may approve, decline or ask for more information. We are not obliged to give reasons but will tell you if a document was unclear or unacceptable.

3.4Badges. "ID Verified" means we matched your ID document to your selfie. "Registered Business" means we checked your company registration documents. Neither is a police or criminal-record check, a licence, a qualification, a guarantee of skill, or insurance. You must not describe a badge as more than this. New vendors may carry a "New vendor" label until they meet our milestones.

3.5You must tell us promptly if any information you gave changes, and we may ask you to re-verify.

4. Your listing

4.1You are responsible for the accuracy of your listing: services, prices in ZAR, durations, availability, address or service area, whether you offer house calls, and photos.

4.2Photos and content must be yours or used with permission, must be an accurate picture of your work and premises, and must not be unlawful, misleading or offensive. You give PriviHub a free, non-exclusive, worldwide licence to display your content on the platform and in marketing of PriviHub for as long as your listing is active and a reasonable period afterwards.

4.3Prices shown must include VAT if you are VAT-registered. Do not charge customers more than the price shown for the booked service, and do not add undisclosed fees.

4.4Keep your calendar up to date. Respond to booking requests within 24 hours; requests that lapse are refunded and count against your response rate.

5. Bookings, standards and safety

5.1Deliver each booked service on time, with reasonable care and skill, as listed. Follow the Booking Policy on cancellation, lateness and no-shows.

5.2Comply with all laws that apply to your activity, including hygiene, health and safety rules, municipal business licensing or zoning where required, product safety, and professional or trade requirements. You are responsible for holding any licence or permit you need. [Confirm Mangaung Metropolitan requirements for salons and home-based operators.]

5.3Carry out patch tests and allergy checks where the service or product calls for them. Do not perform services outside your competence or that need a medical professional.

5.4We recommend, and for house-call or in-salon work may in future require, public liability insurance. [Decide whether insurance is recommended or mandatory.]

5.5House calls. If you offer house calls you must check in through the app on arrival, which records your location and the time; use only the address given in the booking; leave and report if you feel unsafe; and not share the customer's address or details with anyone else. Customer payment for a house call is released to you only once the visit is confirmed by you and the customer, or after the dispute window in the Booking Policy.

5.6Treat customers, other vendors and our staff with respect. Discrimination, harassment, violence and illegal activity result in immediate suspension.

6. Fees, payments and payouts

6.1Customers pay through PriviHub at the time of booking. PriviHub collects that money as your limited payment collection agent and holds it for you until payout, so that a customer paying PriviHub is treated as paying you.

6.2Our fee. PriviHub charges a service fee of [__% of the service price], and the payment processor's fees are [borne by PriviHub / deducted from the payout]. The fee for each booking is shown in your payout statement. [Launch offer: the for-stylists page promises no commission for a vendor's first 3 months. 6.3Payouts are made to the bank account you set up with [Payment Processor], normally within [2 to 5] business days after the service is completed, less our fee, any refunds, and any amount you owe us. We may delay or withhold a payout where we reasonably suspect fraud, where a dispute is open, or where the processor requires further verification.

6.4You must complete the payout setup and identity checks the processor requires. Until then customers cannot pay for your bookings and your listing may be hidden.

6.5Refunds required by the Booking Policy come out of your payout. If a refund is due after you have been paid, you agree that PriviHub may set it off against future payouts or ask you to repay it.

6.6Tax. You are responsible for your own income tax, VAT (if registered) and any other tax on what you earn. Income earned through PriviHub is taxable whether or not your business is registered. PriviHub may be required by law to report payout information to SARS and you consent to this. [Accountant to confirm third-party data reporting obligations.]

7. Customer information

7.1You will receive customers' names and, for house calls, addresses. For this information you are a separate responsible party under POPIA. You may use it only to deliver the booked service and must keep it secure, must not contact customers about other things or sell or share their information, and must delete it when no longer needed.

7.2PriviHub's use of your personal information is explained in the Privacy Policy.

8. Reviews and ratings

8.1Customers may review you. You may not write reviews of yourself, pay for or pressure customers into reviews, or retaliate against a reviewer. You may reply publicly to reviews. We may remove reviews that break our rules, but we will not remove an honest review only because it is negative.

Repeat breach may lead to suspension. Decide with your attorney whether to include this, how long it lasts, and how to word it so it is not an unreasonable restraint.]

10. Suspension and ending

10.1Either of us may end this agreement by [14] days' written notice. We may suspend or end it immediately for serious breach, fraud, a safety incident, loss of a licence you need, or if a verification document proves false.

10.2On ending, pending bookings are cancelled and refunded as the Booking Policy provides, and we pay out what you have earned less amounts you owe. Clauses that by nature continue (fees, tax, liability, information and disputes) continue.

10.3You can delete your vendor account in the app. We keep verification and payment records for the period in the Privacy Policy.

11. Liability and indemnity

11.1Important. You are responsible for your services and conduct, and for loss or injury you cause. You indemnify PriviHub against claims, penalties and costs arising from your services, your breach of this agreement or the law, or content you upload, except to the extent PriviHub's own negligence caused the loss.

11.2PriviHub is not liable for lost income or bookings, loss of profit or any indirect loss. Our total liability to you is limited to the fees we earned from your bookings in the [3] months before the claim. These limits do not apply where the law does not allow them.

11.3PriviHub gives no promise of any minimum number of bookings or income.

12. General

12.1We may change this agreement on [30] days' notice. If you keep listing after that you accept the change; if not, you may end the agreement under clause 10.

12.2South African law governs this agreement and the courts of [Free State Division of the High Court, Bloemfontein] have jurisdiction. Disputes must first go to our support team at [vendors@privihub.app] and, if not resolved in [30] days, to [mediation or arbitration under AFSA rules, or the courts].

12.3This is the whole agreement about your use of the platform, together with the Privacy Policy and Booking Policy. If any clause is invalid the rest continues. A failure to enforce a right is not a waiver. You may not cede or assign this agreement without our written consent.

12.4Notices go to the email address in your account.
