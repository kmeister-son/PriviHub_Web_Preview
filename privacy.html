Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information, and the rights you have.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

1.1PriviHub (Pty) Ltd, registration number [CIPC number] ("PriviHub", "we", "us"), operates a marketplace that lets customers discover and book beauty, barber and spa services from independent service providers ("vendors"), through the PriviHub app (customers), the PriviHub Vendor app (vendors) and the website at [privihub.app] (together, the "Services").

1.2We are the "responsible party" under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA") for the personal information described in this policy. Our registered address is [registered address].

1.3Our Information Officer is [name], reachable at [privacy@privihub.app]. Our Deputy Information Officer is [name].

1.4By creating an account, joining a waitlist or otherwise using the Services you confirm that you have read this policy. Where POPIA requires your consent (for example for identity verification of vendors, or for direct marketing) we ask for it separately, and you may withdraw it at any time.

4. Special personal information, children

4.1Biometric information. When a vendor applicant submits a selfie that is compared with their ID photo, we process biometric information, which POPIA treats as special personal information. We do this only with the applicant's explicit, separate consent, collected in the vendor application, and use it only to verify identity. We delete the selfie [within 90 days of the application decision, or on request if the application is declined] unless a longer period is needed for a dispute or by law.

4.2ID documents. ID document images and numbers are stored in restricted storage, are visible only to staff who review applications, and are kept while you are an active vendor and for [5 years] after your account closes, to meet legal record-keeping duties and resolve disputes.

4.3Children. The Services are for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact the Information Officer and we will delete it.

7. How we protect your information

7.1We take reasonable technical and organisational measures required by POPIA section 19, including: encrypted connections (HTTPS) between the apps and our servers; passwords stored only as salted hashes; session tokens stored in the device's secure storage; short-lived, private links for uploading and viewing images and documents; rate-limiting on sign-in and reset attempts; role-based access so that only authorised staff can see verification documents; and card data handled entirely by our payment processor.

7.2If we have reasonable grounds to believe your information has been accessed by an unauthorised person we will notify the Information Regulator and, where we can identify you, you, as soon as reasonably possible (POPIA section 22).

9. Your rights

9.1You have the right, under POPIA, to: ask whether we hold personal information about you and to receive a copy (access); ask us to correct inaccurate information; ask us to delete or destroy information we are no longer entitled to keep; object to processing based on our legitimate interest; withdraw consent at any time; object to direct marketing; and complain to the Information Regulator.

9.2How to exercise them. Email [privacy@privihub.app] from the email address on your account, or use the options in the app. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We will respond within [30 days]. Access to records can also be requested under PAIA; see our PAIA manual at [privihub.app/paia] if applicable.

9.3Deleting your account. In the app go to [Profile, then Delete account]. You can also request deletion at [privihub.app/delete-account.html] without opening the app. Deleting your account removes your profile and sign-in details. We keep only the booking and payment records we must retain by law, and these are cut off from your identity as far as the law allows. Pending bookings are cancelled and refunded according to the Booking Policy.

10. Marketing

10.1We send marketing messages only to people who have opted in, or to existing customers about similar services where they were given a chance to opt out when their details were collected (POPIA section 69). Every message includes a way to opt out. Service messages about your bookings are not marketing and cannot be switched off while you have an active booking.

11. Location

11.1The customer app asks for your location only to show nearby salons, and only while the app is in use. You can refuse or revoke permission in your phone settings; the app still works and you can browse by suburb or area. The vendor app records location at the moment you check in to a house-call booking and when you set your service address.

12. Cookies and website

12.1See the website notice at privihub.app/legal.html#cookies. In short: our site does not use advertising or analytics cookies at launch. If that changes we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

13. Changes to this policy

13.1We may update this policy. If a change is material we will tell you in the app or by email before it takes effect. The current version and its effective date are always at the top of this page.
