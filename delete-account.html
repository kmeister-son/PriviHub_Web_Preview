Delete your account

How to delete your account, what is removed, and what we are legally required to keep.

How to delete your account

You can delete your PriviHub account from the app: open Profile, then Delete account, and confirm. This works in both the PriviHub and PriviHub Vendor apps.

If you can no longer open the app, email [privacy@privihub.app] from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm your identity and complete the deletion within [30] days.

What is deleted

Your profile, sign-in details, saved addresses and reviews are deleted or anonymised. Vendor accounts also have their listing taken down, and their verification documents and selfie deleted on the schedule set out in our Privacy Policy.

What we keep

We keep booking and payment records for [5 years] because tax and record-keeping law requires it. These are cut off from your name as far as the law allows. Open bookings are cancelled and refunded under our Booking Policy.

Waitlist

To be removed from the launch waitlist, email the same address with the email address or mobile number you used.
