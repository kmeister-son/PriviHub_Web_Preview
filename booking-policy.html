Booking, Cancellation, Refund and Safety Policy

What happens when you book, change or cancel, when you get a refund, and how we keep house calls safe.

1. How a booking works

StepWhat happens
Request or bookYou pick a service, time and (for house calls) address and pay through the app.
Vendor responseSome vendors confirm automatically. Others have 24 hours to accept. If they decline or do not respond, you are refunded in full and the request ends.
ConfirmedYou receive confirmation in the app. The time slot is held for you.
ServiceYou attend (or the vendor visits). House-call visits are checked in on arrival.
CompletedThe vendor is paid, less PriviHub's fee. You can leave a review.

2. Cancelling a booking

When you cancelWhat you get backWhat the vendor receives
Before the vendor acceptsFull refundNothing
After acceptance, more than [24] hours before the start timeFull refundNothing
After acceptance, between [24] hours and [2] hours before[50]% refund[50]% of the service price, less PriviHub's fee
Less than [2] hours before, or you do not arrive (no-show)No refundThe service price, less PriviHub's fee
The vendor cancels, does not respond, or does not arriveFull refund, automaticallyNothing; counts against the vendor's record

2.1You can cancel in the app under Bookings. The time of cancellation is the time recorded by the app.

2.2If you are more than [15] minutes late the vendor may treat the booking as cancelled and the no-show row applies, unless you have agreed a delay in the app's messages.

2.3If something outside your control stops you attending (for example a medical emergency, a death in the family, severe weather or a power failure), contact support with evidence within [48] hours and we will review it fairly. We may still refund in full.

2.4Retained amounts are a reasonable charge for the vendor's lost time and the unfilled slot, as allowed by section 17 of the Consumer Protection Act, and are shown to you before you confirm each booking.

3. Changing a booking

3.1To change the time, cancel and rebook; the cancellation table above applies. [Rescheduling a booking once, free of charge, up to 24 hours before, may be allowed in a later version.]

4. Refunds

4.1Refunds go back to the payment method you used. Banks usually take [5 to 10] business days to show a refund; PriviHub starts it within [2] business days.

4.2If you are unhappy with the quality of a completed service, contact support within [7] days with a short description and photos if relevant. We will speak to the vendor and either arrange a fix, a partial refund or a full refund, where the service was not done with reasonable care and skill. This does not limit your rights under the Consumer Protection Act.

4.3Chargebacks: please contact us first. If a payment is reversed by your bank for a booking that went ahead, we may suspend the account until it is resolved.

5. House calls

5.1The vendor receives your address after the booking is confirmed and uses it only for that visit.

5.2On arrival the vendor checks in through the app, which records the time and location. Payment is released to the vendor after both of you confirm the visit took place, or [48] hours after the appointment if neither of you reports a problem.

5.3If you report that the vendor did not arrive, you are refunded in full while we look into it.

5.4You may cancel a house call up to [24] hours before without charge. Travel time is part of the vendor's slot, so late cancellations follow the table above.

6. Safety

6.1Customers. Check the vendor's badge and reviews. Tell a friend or family member where you are going or that a vendor is visiting. For house calls choose a place you are comfortable in and do not stay alone with the vendor if you feel unsafe. In an emergency call the police on 10111 or an emergency service first, then tell us.

6.2Vendors. Check in at every house call, use only the booked address, leave if you feel unsafe and report it. Do not share customer details.

6.3Report any safety incident to [safety@privihub.app]. We act on reports promptly, which can include suspending an account and, where appropriate, cooperating with the police.

6.4A "Verified" badge shows we checked an identity or a business registration. It is not a guarantee of safety, a background check or a licence.

7. Complaints, reviews and disputes

7.1Contact [support@privihub.app] with your booking details. We will acknowledge within [2] business days. You can also report a review, vendor or customer in the app.

7.2If you are not happy with our resolution you can approach the National Consumer Commission, the relevant ombud or the consumer court.
